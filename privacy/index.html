Privacy Policy

What this website handles and what changes after you follow an external link.

Effective: August 21, 2026

Plain-language summary

This website does not provide user accounts, registrations, subscriptions, payments, newsletters, or a contact form. We do not currently run first-party analytics or advertising scripts. No third-party game service loads on this site; the play buttons are ordinary external links.

Information handled by this website

Like most web hosting services, our hosting provider may process standard request information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as an IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamp, and security or error logs. We do not use this website to ask you for a name, email address, password, payment information, or game account.

Links to Yandex Games

When you select a “Play on Yandex” link, a new tab opens on Yandex Games. From that point, Yandex may receive technical and usage information, use cookies or local storage, show advertising, and offer optional account or game features. Those activities are controlled by Yandex and the game provider, not by escapefromchungachanga.com.

Review the Yandex Privacy Policy and the information shown on Yandex Games before using that separate service.

Local browser features

The site’s small event hook records no personal input and sends nothing to an analytics provider because no analytics service is currently connected.

External links

Links to Yandex Games, Yandex support, and the project issue tracker take you to separate services. Their privacy policies apply after you leave this site.

Children

This site does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Age guidance and suitability for the game are determined by the game and platform providers. Parents and guardians should review the current rating and controls shown by Yandex Games.

Policy changes

If analytics, ads, a contact form, or another data feature is added, this page will be updated before or when that feature goes live. The effective date above shows the current version.

Questions

For a correction about this policy, use the website issue channel listed on the Contact page. For information handled after you leave for Yandex Games, contact Yandex through its official support.
