Site policy

Privacy Policy

What this website does—and what the third-party game frame may do—when you visit.

Effective: August 21, 2026

Plain-language summary

This website does not provide user accounts, registrations, subscriptions, payments, newsletters, or a contact form. We do not currently run first-party analytics or advertising scripts. The playable game is loaded from Yandex Games, a separate third-party service with its own data practices.

Information handled by this website

Like most web hosting services, our hosting provider may process standard request information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as an IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamp, and security or error logs. We do not use this website to ask you for a name, email address, password, payment information, or game account.

The Yandex Games iframe

When the game frame loads, your browser connects directly to Yandex. Yandex may receive technical and usage information, use cookies or local storage, show advertising, and offer optional account or game features. Those activities are controlled by Yandex and the game provider, not by escapefromchungachanga.com.

Review the Yandex Privacy Policy and the information shown inside Yandex Games before using the embedded service. If you prefer not to connect to Yandex, do not load or interact with the game frame.

Local browser features

Fullscreen and reload controls are handled in your browser. The site’s small event hook records no personal input and sends nothing to an analytics provider because no analytics service is currently connected.

External links

Links to Yandex Games, Yandex support, and the project issue tracker take you to separate services. Their privacy policies apply after you leave this site.

Children

This site does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Age guidance and suitability for the game are determined by the game and platform providers. Parents and guardians should review the current rating and controls shown by Yandex Games.

Policy changes

If analytics, ads, a contact form, or another data feature is added, this page will be updated before or when that feature goes live. The effective date above shows the current version.

Questions

For a correction about this policy, use the website issue channel listed on the Contact page. For information handled by the game frame, contact Yandex through its official support.
